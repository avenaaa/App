About CliniScope

A simple, map-based guide to health facilities in Barangay Muzon Proper.

What is CliniScope

Finding the right care, faster.

CliniScope is a web-based directory and map of hospitals, health centers, medical clinics, pharmacies, and dental clinics in Barangay Muzon Proper. It helps residents quickly see what is nearby, what services each facility offers, and whether it is open right now.

Instead of asking around or searching through scattered pages, you can browse everything in one place, from operating hours and contact numbers to directions, so you can decide where to go with confidence.

How to use

Four easy steps

You do not need an account to look up a facility. Just open the map and start exploring.

1

Open the Map

Go to the Map page to see all listed facilities as color-coded pins.

2

Search or Filter

Search by name, or filter by category, service, and location to narrow your options.

3

View Details

Open a facility to see its services, operating hours, contact number, and address.

4

Get Directions

Tap Get Directions to open the route in Google Maps, or call the facility directly.
